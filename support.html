Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

What is an intelligent AI web app?

A web app is an interactive online tool. With appropriate AI features, it can help users find answers, summarise information or complete workflow steps. Capabilities depend on the app's integrations and permissions.

Can an AI assistant speak to customers?

Yes. It can use approved business information to answer common questions and collect enquiries. Clear escalation to a person is important for complex or sensitive questions.

Can you connect to tools we already use?

Often, depending on the services and available integrations or APIs. We can assess email, calendars, spreadsheets and customer management systems during scoping.

Will AI make decisions without staff involvement?

Automation can support defined workflows, but important actions should have permission checks, monitoring and human approval where appropriate.

How do I report a problem?

Use the contact page and choose Technical support. Include the app, a short description, when it happened and steps to reproduce it. Never send passwords or private access keys.
