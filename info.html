First, a few ground rules

Technology should work for people.

Talk about AI and the conversation can swing from feeding hungry children to Terminator robots with red eyes. Everybody seems to have an answer—until a new idea comes along.

♡

Do no deliberate harm

Never use AI to deliberately harm another living being or make someone's life needlessly difficult.

⌁

Use it responsibly

Respect privacy, be honest about AI's role, and keep people involved in important decisions.

✧

Stay curious

Don't get lost in arguments about technology. Focus on the useful things we can learn, build and improve.

A little practice goes a long way

Welcome to Puppy School.

Imagine three puppies: full of potential, eager to learn, but not automatically aware of what you expect. Sometimes they get it right. Sometimes they make a mess.

AI can be similar. Give clear instructions, review the result, explain what needs improving and try again. Teach your assistants what a good result looks like—and check their work.

Don't blindly trust your puppies. Train them. Guide them. Check their work. You remain responsible for important decisions and any actions your AI tools are allowed to take.
