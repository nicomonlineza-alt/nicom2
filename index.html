Choose the right foundation

A website is a start.
An app can do the work.

Both have a place. The right solution depends on what you want your customers or team to achieve.

▤

Static website

A digital brochure: visitors read about your business, explore services and find your contact details.

  • Great for brand presence and clear information
  • Usually simpler and quicker to launch
  • Content changes are generally managed by an editor or developer
  • Can include contact forms and useful integrations
✦

Intelligent web app

An interactive online tool that responds to users, handles data and can connect to AI or business systems.

  • Personalised experiences and user accounts
  • AI chat, recommendations or document assistance
  • Automated workflows across connected tools
  • Dashboards, records and task-specific features
