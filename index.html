Understand the difference

A website shares information.
A web app helps get things done.

These aren't strict categories—modern websites can be dynamic too. The important question is what you need the experience to achieve.

Great for visibility

Traditional / mostly static website

A digital home for your brand, services and information.

  • Explain who you are and what you offer
  • Showcase services, products and contact details
  • Support search visibility and credibility
  • Often relies on people to follow up and handle processes
Built for interaction

Web app with AI integrations

An online tool that can respond, process information and connect systems.

  • Offer an AI sales or support assistant
  • Capture enquiries and help qualify leads
  • Automate repeatable steps across connected tools
  • Provide dashboards, portals and tailored user journeys
Your business may need both.

A strong marketing website can attract people, while a web app helps convert interest into useful action. We'll recommend the right mix—not technology for technology's sake.
